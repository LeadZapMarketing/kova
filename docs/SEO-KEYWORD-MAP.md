# Kova Sun Shade — keyword map and guide list (Malaysia, 19 Oct 2026)

Volumes: Google Ads Keyword Planner via the LeadZap console (`keyword_volume`), monthly searches in
Malaysia, pulled 19 Oct 2026 in English, Malay and Chinese. Beatable slots: `score_serp_beatability`,
19 Oct 2026. Competitors and full tables: [SEO-COMPETITORS.md](SEO-COMPETITORS.md).

Rules for this map:
- One term, one page. **A guide never uses a term a product or service page below already targets.**
- Kova is quote-only: **no prices** on any page or guide (no "RM", no "from", no price ranges).
- Guides are plain and factual, answer the question first, end with the free-quote / WhatsApp call to
  action and link the matching product page. No claims the client has not made on its own site.
