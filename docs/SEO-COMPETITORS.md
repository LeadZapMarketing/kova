# Kova Sun Shade — competitors, beatability and strike order (Malaysia, 19 Oct 2026)

Source: the LeadZap SEO console. **Volumes** = Google Ads Keyword Planner average monthly searches,
Malaysia (`keyword_volume`). **Beatable slots** (0–10) = how many page-one results a new page can take
(forum/UGC, older than 2 years, under 600 words, off-intent, or a weak site), from
`score_serp_beatability` on the live Google Malaysia page one, 19 Oct 2026 (cached 30 days). 3+ slots =
"yours to take". **Strike score** = gap × beatable slots × volume (gap = 1: Kova ranks for none of these
yet, own_rank empty on every term). Competitor ideas: `keyword_ideas` (whole site). Audits:
`audit_competitor` (technical + on-page + PageSpeed). Nothing here is guessed: "no data" means Google
reported nothing, which is not the same as zero.

Kova is quote-only: no prices on any page or guide. Kova sells three products: Roller, Venetian
(aluminium and wood) and VertiSheer. No zebra, roman, outdoor or curtains — those terms are guides at most.

term with a question-led FAQ and FAQPage schema, (2) title ≤ 60 chars with the term up front,
(3) NAP + map link + privacy notice (trust signals they all lack), (4) dated, plain guides.

## Strike order (pages)

1. **/bidai** — bidai 1,900 (5 slots) + bidai tingkap 1,300 (3). H1 and intro lead with "bidai tingkap";
   question-led FAQ (ms.ts `faq.items`) emitted as **FAQPage** schema (missing today); check title ≤ 60.
2. **/venetian** (+ /bidai/venetian) — venetian blinds 1,600 (3) + wooden blinds 590 (4). H1 "Venetian
   Blinds Malaysia: Aluminium & Wood", intro answers "aluminium or wood?", FAQ adds wood-blind questions
   (humidity, kitchens, cleaning), Product schema names both materials.
3. **/vertisheer** — vertical blinds 1,000 (2). Intro and FAQ explain VertiSheer as a vertical sheer
   blind (the term people type); Product schema `category` "Vertical blinds".
4. **/roller** — hold the head term (0 slots). Only the FAQ for blackout roller blinds 210 (1 slot) later.
